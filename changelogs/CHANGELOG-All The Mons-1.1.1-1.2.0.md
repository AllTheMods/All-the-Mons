# 📦 1.2.0

## 📰 General changes and notes

1.2.0 rounds out gym badges for the **BDSP (Sinnoh)** and **Radical Red (Kanto)** series: both the badges and their badge boxes now drop from gym leaders. It also makes every Cobblemon medicine brewable in a brewing stand, and brings a large batch of quest updates.

# 📦 1.2.0

## 📰 General changes and notes

1.2.0 adds **Pika Power**, a new Cobblemon-integrated power block, and rounds out gym badges for the **BDSP (Sinnoh)** and **Radical Red (Kanto)** series - both the badges and their badge boxes now drop from gym leaders. It also makes every Cobblemon medicine brewable in a brewing stand, brings a large batch of quest updates,.

NeoForge updated to 21.1.248.
